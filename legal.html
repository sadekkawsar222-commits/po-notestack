Privacy

  • Your writing stays on your device. Documents, folders and settings are stored in your browser (IndexedDB and local storage). Po Notestack has no server database, no accounts, no analytics and no advertising trackers.
  • Shared links. When you create a share link, the document is compressed and encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in your browser. The key and the encrypted text live only inside the link after the “#”, which browsers never send to any server. Anyone who has the full link (and the password, if you set one) can read that copy. A link cannot be withdrawn once sent; use an expiry date or a password for sensitive text. Expiry is checked by the reader's browser.
  • Third-party requests. To display fonts, the page loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com), and the spelling dictionaries are loaded from jsDelivr (cdn.jsdelivr.net). These services receive your IP address and browser details as part of a normal web request; they never receive your documents. The site is hosted on Vercel, which keeps standard server access logs.
  • Spell checking and Bangla suggestions run entirely in your browser. Your text is not sent anywhere.
  • Clearing data. Clearing your browser's site data for this address permanently deletes your documents. Export important work regularly (PDF, DOCX, Markdown or TXT).
